food spot suggestions in brookyln
(Prospect Heights area)

Cafe de Martini-Breakfast 🍳☕️🥐

Cafe de Martini is a cozy italian cafe in Park Slope. The staff are super lively and welcoming, making sure you get exactly what you need. I always love coming here and order the Matcha and Avocado Toast, which are both great. It's the perfect spot to get work done, to journal, read, or just relax. The interior and exterior create a relaxing and aesthetically pleasing environment
Here are some food and drink suggestions if you're not sure what to order:

    Avocado toast
    Panino Veggie
    Signature Breakfast


BKYLN Crepe-Lunch🍌

BKYLN Crepe is a perfect lunch spot for lovers of crepes, smoothies, grain bowls, and even ice cream! I love going here all the time and they never disapoint. The staff are extremely welcoming and so nice, and give free water (which is strangely hard to comeby now and days) My favorites include:

    the Prospect Heights crepe

    Green Mango Smoothie

    Green mango smoothie!


Geido Brooklyn-Dinner🥢

Geido is a Brooklyn institution, starting in 1985, and still remaining as popular as ever. One of the key special factors about Geido is the fact that the walls are completely free to draw on. I often like to read over the different messages left my others in the past, and it is so fun to read them all. The colorful energy of the restaurant, as well as the ecletic interior design make the space feel unchanged from the 80s. The food is the perfect pick me up if you're ever craving homecooked style Japanese food. I would specifically reccomend:

    Vegetable Udon Soup
    Lotus Root Side
    Green Tea Ice Cream
